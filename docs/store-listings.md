
### Short description — max 80 characters (this text is 69)
```

```

### Full description — max 4000 characters
```
MadGigz is a for-you page for local live music. Scroll reels from independent artists playing near you — and when a show catches your eye, buy the ticket in the same swipe.

No more hunting. No jumping from a post, to a bio link, to a ticket site, to your email. On MadGigz the reel, the show and the ticket all live in one place. Your ticket lands straight in the app, with a QR code and an Apple Wallet or Google Wallet pass ready for the door.

FOR FANS
• A feed of reels, not a wall of listings — discover artists by watching, not by reading text.
• See what's on this week in your city.
• Buy tickets in seconds. Your QR ticket is always in your pocket.
• Add tickets to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.
• Follow the artists you love and never miss their next show.
• Built for local, independent live music — the gigs actually happening around you, not stadium tours.

FOR ARTISTS
• Post your show and link your reels — fans discover you by watching.
• Sell tickets in the app. No gatekeeping, no curation queue.
• Get paid out directly to your bank account.
• A simple, fair 5% fee, shown openly — and you're always free to keep selling through your own links too.
• Scan tickets at the door, right from your phone.

Madrid first. More cities soon.

MadGigz — the local gig, in your pocket.
```

- **Advertising Identifier (IDFA):** No (the app does not use it)
